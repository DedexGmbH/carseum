Putting it together

F10 vent clip

  1. Put the board in the round case, screen first, USB-C at the bottom. Snap the back on.
  2. Slide the clip's dovetail sideways into the slot on the back of the cup.
  3. Press the case into the cup, the USB hole over the notch at the bottom.
  4. Plug in a right-angle USB-C cable so it bends back into the vent.
  5. Push the clip onto one vent slat and run the cable to a USB socket.

Print: PETG black, 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 40% infill, no supports. About 25 g.

E93 flush insert

  1. Buy a used centre vent (BMW 64229130458) and measure its driver-side outlet.
  2. Print the 20-minute fit test and check it sits flush and clicks in.
  3. Print the insert, ring and cover in matte black ASA or PETG.
  4. Board in, clamp ring on with two M2 screws, cable through the cover, seal with silicone.
  5. Click the insert into the spare vent and swap the vents, moving the hazard switch plug across.

Sizes are estimates until the spare vent is measured. Print the fit test first.
