BMW F10 · left-hand drive · clip-on

Vent clip

A cup holds the round Dash case, angled 10° towards the driver. A clip grips one horizontal vent slat, like a phone holder. No measuring, no tools, and it comes off without a trace. It fits most cars with horizontal slats; there's a cup for each side of the car.

Ø 50.6Cup outside
10°Towards the driver
≈ 27Out from the vent
1.8Clip gap (slats 1.5–3)

Build

  1. Put the board in the round case, screen first, USB-C at the bottom. Snap the back on.
  2. Slide the clip's dovetail sideways into the slot on the back of the cup.
  3. Press the case into the cup until it clicks, with the USB hole over the notch at the bottom.
  4. Plug in a right-angle USB-C cable so it bends back into the vent.
  5. Push the clip straight onto one vent slat, then run the cable to a USB socket.

Print: PETG black, 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 40% infill, no supports. About 25 g. Loose on the slat? Reprint just the clip with a smaller slat_gap.

BMW E93 · right-hand drive · built in

Flush vent insert

The screen sits flat in the face of the driver's centre vent outlet, like a factory display, while the rest of the outlet keeps working slats. The E9x centre vent is one part with the hazard button (BMW 64229130458), so the insert goes into a used spare vent and the original stays untouched. The screen side is sealed off from the vent's hot and cold air.

Sizes are estimates. Measure a spare vent first and print the 20-minute fit test before the real insert. Print the insert in matte black ASA or PETG, not PLA.
